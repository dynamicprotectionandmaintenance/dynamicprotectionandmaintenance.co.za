About Us

More than a service provider — a trusted long-term partner.

Dynamic Protection & Maintenance (Pty) Ltd is a professional, client-focused property maintenance service provider founded by women with a vision to deliver reliable, practical and high-quality protection and maintenance solutions.

We focus on quality workmanship, preventative care, project management and client satisfaction. Based in Durban, KwaZulu-Natal, we support clients with dependable services tailored to their property, operational needs and budget.
